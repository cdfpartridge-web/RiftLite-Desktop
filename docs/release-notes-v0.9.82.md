# RiftLite v0.9.82

Radiance pre-season statistics are here, alongside a simpler setup flow and replay improvements.

- **Radiance pre-season:** New default season from 10 October 2026 at 17:35:38 UK time across personal history, community reports, decks, teams and replay libraries. Earlier seasons remain available. A highlight box explains the change; old matches uploaded later stay in their original season.
- **Quick setup:** A five-step welcome replaces the guided tour, covering your player name, Atlas/TCG Arena, account, replays and deck. Replay preferences stay inside setup. Includes Piltover Archive import guidance, an Atlas toolbar overview, F11 and the replay website.
- **RiftLite accounts:** Discord is available for sign-up as well as sign-in. New profiles default to Public replays; existing privacy choices are preserved.
- **Fullscreen and Game log:** An optional fullscreen top bar keeps Play controls accessible. Atlas Game log Copy uses the desktop clipboard, and non-game placeholder activity is filtered out.
- **Replay improvements:** Faster cache updates and atomic saves, MP4 duration validation based on video timestamps, and clearer explanations for incomplete captures. Web Replays show the latest Atlas status indicators, including counted buffs.
- **Cards:** Added 80 prints since v0.9.81, including 68 Radiance prints. The catalog now has 1,445 prints, with updated names and artwork.

This release also adds passive diagnostics to help investigate Atlas reconnects; it does not claim a confirmed connection fix. Opening Turns Lab and Replay Coach remain Coming soon. Your existing history and preferences are preserved.
