RIFTLITE BETA v0.9.75
Fullscreen and Atlas artwork

More room in fullscreen
Fullscreen Play hides RiftLite's toolbar and navigation so your game fills the screen. Press F11 to return to the normal layout.

Keep your game in place
Entering and leaving fullscreen preserves the open game, zoom and normal navigation preference. Holding F11 no longer repeatedly switches modes.

Smoother Atlas card artwork
Removed an older image filter that could make text and artwork edges look uneven. This applies to standard and alternate artwork. Atlas still supplies the same card images; positions, rotations and controls are preserved.

Existing profiles, matches, decks, replays and settings are retained.
Available for Windows, Mac Intel and Mac Apple Silicon.
Mac installers remain ad-hoc signed and are not Apple-notarized.
