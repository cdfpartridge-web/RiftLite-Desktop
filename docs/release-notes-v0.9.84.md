# RiftLite v0.9.84

## Atlas match logger fix

Fixed a fresh Match logger opening when starting another Atlas game after saving the previous match. RiftLite now recognizes the finished board even after Atlas closes its result dialog, while continuing to capture real rematches and BO3 games.

Also fixed a delayed review request reopening a popup after saving, deferring or deleting a review, or starting a new game.

Existing accounts, settings, decks, matches and replays are preserved. Includes the Atlas sign-in fix from v0.9.83.
