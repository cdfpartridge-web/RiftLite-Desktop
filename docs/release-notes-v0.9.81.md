# RiftLite v0.9.81

- **Change past replay visibility:** use Change visibility on a saved game or in My replays, choose Public, Unlisted or Private, then save. Desktop sign-in now works with these controls, and future-upload defaults stay separate from completed replays.
- **Safer automatic Discord delivery:** automatic posts preserve Public replays and stop if you have made a queued replay Private. You can still choose Share to Discord explicitly later.
- **Known opponent hand:** modern Atlas reveal effects now populate the desktop panel and overlay. Revealed cards remain as a memory aid until they leave; unknown draws stay hidden.
- **66 additional card prints:** 40 Radiance previews and 26 previously missing alternate arts, promos and tokens. Includes Evelynn's standard and showcase legends, signed Mordekaiser, new battlefields and alternate battlefield artwork.
- **Correct alternate artwork:** captures and replays recognise alternate and signed legends and preserve their printed artwork.

Available for Windows, Mac Apple Silicon and Mac Intel. Existing recordings, settings and account connections are preserved. Opening Turns Lab and Replay Coach remain Coming soon.
