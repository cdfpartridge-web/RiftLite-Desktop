# RiftLite Beta v0.9.75 - Fullscreen and Atlas artwork

- **More room in fullscreen.** Fullscreen Play hides RiftLite's toolbar and navigation and lets the game fill the screen. Press F11 to return to the normal layout.
- **Keep your game in place.** Entering and leaving fullscreen preserves the open game, zoom and normal navigation preference. Holding F11 no longer repeatedly switches modes.
- **Smoother Atlas card artwork.** Removed an older image filter that could make text and artwork edges look uneven. Standard and alternate artwork use Atlas's native image rendering; the card images, positions, rotations and controls are preserved.

Existing profiles, matches, decks, replays and settings are retained. This update is available for Windows, Mac Intel and Mac Apple Silicon.

macOS installers remain ad-hoc signed and are not Apple-notarized.
