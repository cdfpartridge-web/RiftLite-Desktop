# RiftLite v0.9.87

- Completed Atlas games can automatically add All cards to your interactive replay, while keeping RiftLite's original opening capture and Player view.
- Supported replays show the opponent's verified kept opening cards and mulligan replacements. Cards Atlas cannot identify remain Unknown. All cards follows the replay's access settings and can be turned off for other viewers; private review notes stay private.
- Fixed automatic collection for some completed BO1 games that do not send Atlas's usual ending signal. Collection waits until gameplay has finished and Atlas confirms the completed replay.
- Reduced background work during Atlas play and moved active replay journals to local app storage before saving completed captures to your chosen folder. Replay recovery and deferred collection are more reliable.

Existing accounts, matches, decks, settings and recordings are preserved. These changes address measured sources of overhead; they do not establish that every possible connection or gameplay pause has been resolved.
