# RiftLite Beta v0.9.76 — Deck practice and game logs

- **Practise with your actual deck.** My active deck in Mulligan Lab deals unlimited random opening hands from your saved card list. Set aside your Chosen Champion, keep your hand or redraw up to two cards, and see the result. Practice works without community data and never switches to another deck or grades your choices.
- **Read your Atlas game logs.** The new Game log button in match history opens retained text logs, with search, copy and separate game tabs for best-of-three matches. Availability depends on the capture retained for that match.
- **Choose your dates.** Filter matches, replays, personal statistics, Deck Insights and other data reports by a specific date or date range.
- **Training and replay fixes.** Valid Mulligan Lab packs no longer fail just because the server and desktop card catalogs have different totals. A fresh Atlas Game 1 is also kept separate from stale captures from an earlier room.

Existing profiles, matches, decks, replays and settings are retained. Available for Windows, Mac Intel and Mac Apple Silicon.

macOS installers remain ad-hoc signed and are not Apple-notarized.
