# RiftLite v0.9.86

- Official Radiance is now the default season across personal and community stats, deck analysis and replay filters. It starts on 23 October 2026 at 13:11:27 UK time. Radiance pre-season and older history remain available; the new reminder explains the change.
- Interactive replays lead Quick setup, Review and Recording & sharing. Fresh installs capture interactive data with video and timed screenshots off. Existing preferences are preserved, and uploads and sharing remain optional.
- Repeated text or Piltover Archive deck imports offer Update existing or Save as separate deck, allowing named copies with independent stats.
- Video recordings stay in local app storage while recording, then move to the chosen folder when complete. This reduces ongoing writes into synced folders such as OneDrive.

Existing accounts, matches, decks and videos are preserved. The recording-storage change does not establish or resolve every reported Atlas gameplay pause; that investigation remains open.
