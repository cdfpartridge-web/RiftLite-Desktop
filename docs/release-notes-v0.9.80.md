# RiftLite v0.9.80

- Set up interactive web replays, local video, microphone recording and Discord in one **Recording & sharing** page. Your existing capture and privacy choices are preserved.
- Browse saved games in **Replays & videos**, with replay, video and game-log availability shown separately. Game details bring the summary, replay, decks and sharing actions together.
- Recover interrupted uploads with clearer activity, retry and **Keep local only** actions. Discord sharing now has a scrollable destination picker and clearer setup guidance; failed Discord posts do not restart a completed replay upload.
- Find games saved for later under **Matches → Needs review**, then save the reviewed result to finish them. Older-season games are included in this queue.
- Manage account settings, private Teams, Hubs and invitations more clearly. Recording indicators now sit in the existing Play top bar.
- Reduced repeated background database reads, replay-list rebuilding and legend-name processing for smoother navigation and status updates. Replay processing waits until you open the replay tab.
- Added automatic local crash diagnostics and an export option for troubleshooting. Diagnostic files stay on your device unless you choose to share an export.
- Added 22 Radiance card prints covering 18 new names, including **Mordekaiser, Iron Revenant**, **Bandle Scouts' Academy**, **Black Market** and **Hunter's Circle**, plus alternate and signed artwork. Existing replay identities and the earlier Lost to the Sand name remain supported.

The card catalog includes reveals audited on **1 October 2026**. Opening Turns Lab and Replay Coach remain marked **Coming soon**.
