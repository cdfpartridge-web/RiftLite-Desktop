# RiftLite Beta v0.9.30

RiftLite v0.9.30 brings together the major improvements made since v0.9.10, with a focus on Web Replays, local recordings, RiftAtlas reliability, and everyday replay management.

## Web Replays

- A clearer Web Replays centre now handles setup, upload status, visibility, Discord sharing, and recovery in one place.
- RiftAtlas and TCGA uploads can be enabled separately, with new uploads starting Private by default.
- Failed or interrupted uploads recover more reliably, with simple options to retry, upload a usable replay with a missing opening mulligan, or keep it local only.
- Healthy upload diagnostics now collapse neatly so the replay library keeps most of the screen.
- Web Replays now include **Take Control**, letting you explore alternative plays without changing the original replay.
- Public replay browsing and account-linked replay libraries are more reliable and easier to navigate.

## Replay library and recordings

- Rename replays directly inside RiftLite.
- Organise replays into in-app folders, mark favourites, filter the library, and delete multiple replays at once.
- TCGA game sound can now be included in recordings and combined with optional microphone audio.
- Interrupted recordings are preserved and recovered automatically where possible.
- On Windows, MP4 exports now include selected flags, notes, and drawings instead of silently exporting a clean video.

## RiftAtlas improvements

- A new opponent-hand memory panel remembers cards legitimately revealed during play, using real card art. Open it from the toolbar or with `F12`.
- RiftAtlas loading and sign-in problems now have connection checks, safer repair options, and a copyable support report in Settings.
- Match and room transitions are handled more reliably, reducing missing games, ghost matches, and repeated post-match forms.
- Keyboard focus and text entry inside the embedded RiftAtlas window have also been improved.

## Matches, prep, and stats

- Vendetta is now the default current season.
- Season filters are available across personal history, personal stats, decks, teams, and private hubs, while older seasons remain available.
- The play widget is now labelled **Prep/Notes** and can be turned on or off for both RiftAtlas and TCGA.
- BO3 handling and TCGA result, battlefield, counter, attachment, and score tracking have received further reliability improvements.

## Accounts and community

- Google, email, and Discord account recovery is more dependable.
- Cloud backup and restore are safer when using multiple devices or switching accounts.
- Opted-in public player profiles are easier to discover and can show match history, deck information, stats, and Public Web Replays.
- Discord replay delivery and private-hub membership controls are more reliable.

Existing accounts, settings, matches, decks, and replay data remain intact. Local replay videos remain on the device unless Web Replay uploading is explicitly enabled.
