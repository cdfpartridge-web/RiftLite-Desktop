# Radiance catalog scan — 1 October 2026

**Published 2 October 2026:** This audited catalog is now live in desktop **v0.9.80** and the matching replay website. [Release receipt](./RELEASE-2026-10-02.md). The remaining sections record the original local audit.

The original 1 October audit was a local source update, with no version change, publication or deployment at that point.

## Source audit

Fetched the full three-page [Piltover Archive Radiance library](https://piltoverarchive.com/cards?sets=RAD) and [Riot's official gallery](https://playriftbound.com/en-us/card-gallery/) at approximately 16:38 UTC on 1 October. Piltover supplied 107 distinct print records; Riot supplied 84 Radiance records. Their union contains **110 Radiance print identities**, up from 88 in the 30 September release. Riot's other sets contained no unsupported print identities.

The **22 additions cover 18 previously unsupported card names**: eight spell prints, five unit/champion prints, one gear, four legend prints, three battlefields and one rune variant. The new legend is **Mordekaiser, Iron Revenant**; the new battlefields are **Bandle Scouts' Academy**, **Black Market**, and **Hunter's Circle**. The additions also include Akali, Brash; Graves, Blasting Through; Evelynn, In Control; and Ekko's overnumbered and signed artwork. Collector identities and different artwork remain distinct.

All 22 new image URLs returned HTTP 200 with image content types. Their printed English card images were inspected for names, types, costs and collector numbers. The printed title **Lost to the Sands** also corrects the earlier RAD-013 metadata; the previous name, Lost to the Sand, remains an alias and its image is unchanged. No unrevealed standard version of the Evelynn promo or Body rune is invented.

## Desktop implementation

The existing guarded registry updater produced **1,299 prints** and retained every previous print identity. The only existing normalized record change is the RAD-013 title and compatibility alias. All other 1,276 prior records are unchanged. The overlay and required-print expectations retain these previews if the main feed remains behind the reveal sources.

The legend picker, artwork mapping, Fury/Order deck theme, battlefield catalog and TCGA battlefield text matcher include the new setup cards. Exact known preview URLs now identify a legend even when the image has no collector code or Riot hash in its filename. Bandle Scouts' Academy is matched before the older generic Academy alias. Existing shared collector parsing already supports promo codes such as RAD-SP3 and signed codes.
